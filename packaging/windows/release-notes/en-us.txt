What's new in version 0.2:
• English interface: GeoLearner follows the system language, which you can change on the home screen. Voice input is available in English too.
• Three new quizzes: provinces and territories of Canada, states and territories of Australia, and French department numbers (no map, both ways).
• "By number" zones to learn the French departments ten at a time.
• New home screen with quiz cards; each quiz has its own page with your progress.
• Explore the map freely, without a quiz, with every place named.
• Mastery of each place (learned, in progress, to review), mastery map and review games.
• Second chance in Name and Find modes: a missed place comes back a few questions later (can be turned off).
• Locator map for the insets (French overseas departments, Alaska, Hawaii).
